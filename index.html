Haohao Sun

I recently started as a postdoctoral researcher at am at the School of Atmospheric Sciences, Nanjing University. I received my BSc in Atmospheric Science and completed my PhD in 2026 under the supervision of Dr. Lili Lei. at Nanjing University, where my doctoral research focused on paleoclimate reconstruction using ensemble data assimilation methods. My research interests include climate field reconstruction, multiscale data assimilation, and the integration of artificial intelligence and data assimilation. I am currently working on reconstructions of the Last Millennium and the Holocene to improve our understanding of climate variability and dynamics across timescales.

I can be reached out by hhsun@nju.edu.cn.
